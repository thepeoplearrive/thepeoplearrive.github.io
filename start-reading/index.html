Before you begin

What you are walking into.

The People Arrive is fiction about civic life: what people build together when familiar systems stop serving them.

It begins in Grainville, on the day the coupons stop working. There is no single villain and no single hero. There are neighbors, folding chairs, and a table that keeps getting longer.
